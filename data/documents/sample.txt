Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) combines a retriever component with a generative
language model. Instead of relying solely on parameters learned during training, the
system retrieves relevant chunks of text from an external knowledge base at inference
time and conditions the generation on that retrieved context. This reduces hallucination
and allows the system to cite sources, since the model's output can be grounded in
specific retrieved passages rather than purely memorized knowledge.
